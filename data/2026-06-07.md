### [1] [The free-streaming length of dark matter from JWST observations of 28 strong gravitational lenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05277)
*D. Gilman,A. M. Nierenberg,T. Treu,K. N. Abazajian,T. Anguita,V. N. Bennert,A. J. Benson,S. Birrer,S. G. Djorgovski,X. Du,C. Gannon,S. F. Hoenig,R. E. Keeley,A. Kusenko,H. R. Larsson,M. Malkan,T. Morishita,V. Motta,L. A. Moustakas,P. Mozumdar,H. Paugnat,W. Sheu,D. Sluse,D. Stern,M. Stiavelli,D. Williams,K. C. Wong*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: This paper uses JWST observations of 28 quadruple-image strong gravitational lenses to measure dark matter halo properties and constrain the free-streaming length $λ_{\rm{FS}}$. By jointly modeling lensed arcs, image positions, and magnifications, the authors improve sensitivity to subhalo perturbations. They exclude warm dark matter (WDM) models that deviate from cold dark matter (CDM) predictions above halo masses of $10^{7.2}–10^{7.4} M_{\odot}$, translating to upper bounds on $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ and lower limits on thermal relic particle mass ($m_{\rm{therm}} > 6.5–7.4 \ \rm{keV}$). Assuming CDM, they detect a projected subhalo mass density consistent with simulations.
\n\n
本文利用JWST对28个四像强引力透镜系统的观测，测量暗物质（DM）晕的性质并限制其自由流动长度 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$。通过联合建模延展的透镜弧、像位置和相对放大率，作者提高了对子晕扰动的灵敏度。他们排除了在晕质量高于 $10^{7.2}–10^{7.4} M_{\odot}$ 时偏离冷暗物质（CDM）预测的温暗物质（WDM）模型，对应于对 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ 的上限和热遗迹粒子质量的下限（$m_{\rm{therm}} > 6.5–7.4 \ \rm{keV}$）。在假设CDM的前提下，他们测得椭圆星系周围子晕的投影面密度与数值模拟一致。

Motivation: Theoretical models of particle dark matter predict the existence of gravitationally bound overdensities known as halos, whose abundance and internal structure depend critically on the DM particle's free-streaming length $λ_{\rm{FS}}$, which in turn is determined by the particle mass and production mechanism. Cold dark matter (CDM) predicts abundant low-mass halos, while warm or lighter DM suppresses small-scale structure below a cutoff scale related to $λ_{\rm{FS}}$. Strong gravitational lensing is uniquely sensitive to such sub-galactic DM structures, making it an ideal probe to test deviations from CDM and constrain DM particle properties.
\n\n
粒子暗物质理论模型预言了被称为晕（halos）的引力束缚超密结构，其丰度和内部结构强烈依赖于暗物质粒子的自由流动长度 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$，而后者由粒子质量和产生机制决定。冷暗物质（CDM）预言存在大量低质量晕，而温暗物质或更轻的暗物质会在与 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ 相关的截断尺度以下抑制小尺度结构。强引力透镜对这类亚星系尺度的暗物质结构具有独特敏感性，因此是检验CDM偏离并约束暗物质粒子性质的理想探针。

Method: The authors analyze 28 quadruply imaged strong lens systems observed by JWST. They perform a joint reconstruction of both the extended lensed arcs and the positions and relative magnifications of multiple images. This combined approach enhances sensitivity to gravitational perturbations induced by line-of-sight and host-halo substructures (i.e., DM subhalos). Using this high-fidelity lens modeling, they statistically compare the observed perturbations to predictions from cosmological $N$-body simulations and semi-analytic models of subhalo populations under different DM scenarios (CDM vs. WDM). Bayesian inference is used to place constraints on $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ and the thermal relic mass $m_{\rm{therm}}$.
\n\n
作者分析了JWST观测的28个四重像强透镜系统。他们对延展的透镜弧以及多重像的位置和相对放大率进行联合重建。这种组合方法增强了对视线方向和主晕内子结构（即暗物质子晕）所引起的引力扰动的灵敏度。利用这种高保真度的透镜建模，他们将观测到的扰动与宇宙学 $N$-body 模拟和半解析模型在不同暗物质情形（CDM 与 WDM）下预测的子晕分布进行统计比较。通过贝叶斯推断，对自由流动长度 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ 和热遗迹粒子质量 $m_{\rm{therm}}$ 施加限制。

Result: The analysis excludes deviations from CDM predictions for subhalo masses above $10^{7.2} M_{\odot}$ (based on $N$-body simulations) and $10^{7.4} M_{\odot}$ (based on semi-analytic models). These correspond to upper limits on the free-streaming length: $λ_{\rm{FS}} < 6.0 \ \rm{kpc}$ and $λ_{\rm{FS}} < 7.0 \ \rm{kpc}$, respectively. For a spin--1/2 thermal relic DM particle, this implies lower bounds on its mass: $m_{\rm{therm}} > 7.4 \ \rm{keV}$ and $m_{\rm{therm}} > 6.5 \ \rm{keV}$. Under the assumption of negligible $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ (i.e., CDM), the projected surface mass density of subhalos around elliptical galaxies is measured as $1.7_{-1.2}^{+2.6} \times 10^7 \ \mathrm{M}_{\odot} \ \rm{kpc^{-2}}$ at 95% confidence, consistent with CDM expectations.
\n\n
分析排除了在子晕质量高于 $10^{7.2} M_{\odot}$（基于 $N$-body 模拟）和 $10^{7.4} M_{\odot}$（基于半解析模型）时对CDM预测的偏离。这分别对应于自由流动长度的上限：$λ_{\rm{FS}} < 6.0 \ \rm{kpc}$ 和 $λ_{\rm{FS}} < 7.0 \ \rm{kpc}$。对于自旋为1/2的热遗迹暗物质粒子，这意味着其质量下限为：$m_{\rm{therm}} > 7.4 \ \rm{keV}$ 和 $m_{\rm{therm}} > 6.5 \ \rm{keV}$。在假设 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ 可忽略（即CDM情形）下，椭圆星系周围子晕的投影面密度在95%置信度下测得为 $1.7_{-1.2}^{+2.6} \times 10^7 \ \mathrm{M}_{\odot} \ \rm{kpc^{-2}}$，与CDM预期一致。

Conclusion: The results strongly support the cold dark matter paradigm by confirming its prediction of abundant low-mass subhalos down to $\sim 10^7 M_{\odot}$. The tight constraints on $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ and $m_{\rm{therm}}$ rule out significant portions of the parameter space for warm dark matter models, particularly those with keV-scale thermal relics lighter than $\sim 6.5 \ \rm{keV}$. The successful detection of subhalo-induced lensing perturbations using JWST data demonstrates the power of high-resolution strong lensing as a probe of dark matter microphysics.
\n\n
结果强有力地支持冷暗物质范式，证实了其对低至 $\sim 10^7 M_{\odot}$ 质量尺度上丰富子晕的预言。对 $λ_{\rm{FS}}$ 和 $m_{\rm{therm}}$ 的严格限制排除了温暗物质模型参数空间的很大一部分，特别是热遗迹粒子质量低于 $\sim 6.5 \ \rm{keV}$ 的keV尺度模型。利用JWST数据成功探测到由子晕引起的透镜扰动，展示了高分辨率强引力透镜作为暗物质微观物理探针的强大能力。

Abstract: The formation of gravitationally bound overdensities of dark matter (DM), or \textit{halos}, is a generic prediction of theories with particle DM. We present a measurement of halo properties in 28 quadruple image strong lens systems recently observed by JWST, and use these observations to constrain the free-streaming length, $λ_{\rm{FS}}$, of DM, a quantity that depends on the DM particle mass and formation mechanism. We improve on previous lensing analyses by simultaneously reconstructing extended lensed arcs with image positions and relative magnifications, enhancing sensitivity to perturbations by halos. Our analysis rules out deviations from the predictions of cold dark matter (CDM) on scales above $10^{7.2} M_{\odot}$ and $10^{7.4} M_{\odot}$ for subhalo abundance predicted by cosmological $N$-body simulations and semi-analytic models, respectively. These bounds correspond to upper limits $λ_{\rm{FS}}<6.0 \ \rm{kpc}$ and $λ_{\rm{FS}}<7.0 \ \rm{kpc}$, and lower limits on the mass of a spin--1/2 thermal relic DM particle $m_{\rm{therm}}>7.4 \ \rm{keV}$ and $m_{\rm{therm}}>6.5 \ \rm{keV}$. Conversely, assuming a negligible free-streaming length, as predicted by CDM, we measure a projected mass in subhalos around elliptical galaxies $1.7_{-1.2}^{+2.6} \times 10^7 \ \mathrm{M}_{\odot} \ \rm{kpc^{-2}}$ at $95 \%$ confidence. These inferences confirm key predictions of the CDM paradigm.

### [2] [AGN Line-Intensity Mapping: A Probe of Faint Black Holes at Cosmic Noon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05301)
*Eli Visbal,Greg L. Bryan*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: This paper proposes line-intensity mapping (LIM) of the [Ne V] emission line as a novel method to detect the cumulative signal from active galactic nuclei (AGN), including those too faint to be individually resolved. By cross-correlating [Ne V] intensity maps with galaxy redshift surveys, the authors demonstrate high signal-to-noise detection prospects for future instruments like CDIM and PRIMA, leveraging the high ionization potential of [Ne V] (97.1 eV) as a clean AGN tracer. The technique accesses obscured AGN via the narrow line region and remains sensitive below traditional detection thresholds, enabling new constraints on AGN emissivity, black hole–halo connections, and seeding mechanisms.
\n\n
本文提出利用[Ne V]发射线的线强度映射（Line-Intensity Mapping, LIM）作为探测活动星系核（AGN）的新方法，尤其适用于那些单个源低于探测阈值的情况。通过将[Ne V]强度图与星系红移巡天交叉关联，作者展示了未来CDIM和PRIMA类仪器具有高信噪比的探测前景，这得益于[Ne V]高达97.1 eV的电离势，使其成为几乎无污染的AGN示踪剂。该技术通过窄线区可探测高度遮蔽的AGN，并对传统探测阈值以下的AGN仍保持灵敏度，从而为AGN辐射历史、黑洞-晕关联以及超大质量黑洞种子机制等科学问题提供新约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Current AGN surveys are limited by sensitivity thresholds that exclude faint or heavily obscured populations, leading to incomplete census of AGN activity across cosmic time. Traditional direct detection misses sources below $5\\sigma$ limits, especially at high redshifts. The authors aim to overcome this limitation by using line-intensity mapping (LIM)—a statistical technique that measures cumulative emission from unresolved sources—combined with cross-correlation against galaxy surveys to isolate the AGN contribution. The choice of [Ne V] is motivated by its uniquely high ionization potential (97.1 eV), which ensures it arises almost exclusively from AGN narrow-line regions, even in Compton-thick systems, making it an ideal clean tracer.
\n\n
当前AGN巡天受限于灵敏度阈值，无法探测较暗或高度遮蔽的AGN群体，导致对宇宙时空中AGN活动的统计不完整。传统的直接探测方法会遗漏低于$5\\sigma$极限的源，尤其在高红移处更为严重。作者旨在通过线强度映射（LIM）——一种测量未分辨源累积辐射的统计技术——并结合与星系巡天的交叉关联，以分离出AGN贡献，从而克服这一限制。选择[Ne V]是因为其极高的电离势（97.1 eV），确保其几乎仅来自AGN的窄线区，即使在Compton厚系统中亦如此，因此是理想的无污染示踪剂。

Method: The authors employ a Fisher matrix formalism to forecast the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) for detecting the cross-correlation between [Ne V] line-intensity maps and galaxy redshift surveys. They consider two hypothetical future instruments: (1) a CDIM-like optical/near-IR spectrometer targeting the [Ne V] $\\lambda3426$ Å line at $z\\sim2{-}3$, and (2) a PRIMA-like mid-IR instrument targeting the [Ne V] $14.3\\,\\mu$m line. The key observable is the cross-power spectrum $P_{\\rm x}(k,\\mu)$, where $\\mu$ is the line-of-sight cosine. Redshift-space distortions (RSD) break degeneracies between the mean intensity $S_{\\rm NeV}$ and linear bias $b_{\\rm NeV}$, allowing separate constraints. The model incorporates AGN luminosity functions, duty cycles, and clustering bias, and accounts for instrumental noise, survey volume, and resolution.
\n\n
作者采用Fisher矩阵形式预测[Ne V]线强度图与星系红移巡天交叉关联的信噪比（S/N）。他们考虑两种假想的未来仪器：(1) 类CDIM的光学/近红外光谱仪，针对红移$z\\sim2{-}3$处的[Ne V] $\\lambda3426$ Å谱线；(2) 类PRIMA的中红外仪器，针对[Ne V] $14.3\\,\\mu$m谱线。关键观测量是交叉功率谱$P_{\\rm x}(k,\\mu)$，其中$\\mu$为视线方向余弦。红移空间畸变（RSD）打破平均强度$S_{\\rm NeV}$与线性偏置$b_{\\rm NeV}$之间的简并，从而实现分别约束。模型包含AGN光度函数、占空比和成团偏置，并计入仪器噪声、巡天体积和分辨率。

Result: For the CDIM-like instrument, the product $S_{\\rm NeV} b_{\\rm NeV}$ is constrained with high significance ($S/N \\sim 9{-}4$) across $z=2{-}3$, with RSD enabling individual measurements of $S_{\\rm NeV}$ and $b_{\\rm NeV}$. Approximately 10% of the total [Ne V] LIM signal originates from AGN below the $5\\sigma$ direct detection threshold ($L_{\\rm bol} \\sim 5\\times10^{43}$ erg s$^{-1}$ at $z=3$), and this sub-threshold population is detectable at $S/N = 9{-}4$. The PRIMA-like instrument yields slightly lower S/N due to instrumental characteristics but provides a dust-insensitive probe via the $14.3\\,\\mu$m line, offering complementary information. Both instruments access AGN populations missed by current surveys, particularly obscured and low-luminosity systems.
\n\n
对于类CDIM仪器，乘积$S_{\\rm NeV} b_{\\rm NeV}$在$z=2{-}3$范围内被高显著性约束（$S/N \\sim 9{-}4$），红移空间畸变（RSD）使得$S_{\\rm NeV}$和$b_{\\rm NeV}$可被单独测量。总[Ne V] LIM信号中约10%来自低于$5\\sigma$直接探测阈值的AGN（在$z=3$时对应$L_{\\rm bol} \\sim 5\\times10^{43}$ erg s$^{-1}$），该阈下群体的探测信噪比为$S/N = 9{-}4$。类PRIMA仪器因仪器特性导致信噪比略低，但通过不受尘埃消光影响的$14.3\\,\\mu$m谱线提供互补探针。两种仪器均可探测当前巡天遗漏的AGN群体，尤其是遮蔽型和低光度系统。

Conclusion: Line-intensity mapping of [Ne V] offers a powerful, statistically robust method to probe the full AGN population—including faint, obscured, and high-redshift sources—beyond the reach of conventional surveys. The high ionization potential of [Ne V] ensures minimal contamination, while cross-correlation with galaxy surveys and inclusion of redshift-space distortions enable precise cosmological and astrophysical constraints. Future LIM experiments targeting [Ne V] can address fundamental questions such as the total AGN emissivity history, the black hole–halo mass relation at low luminosities, and the origin of supermassive black holes through discrimination of seeding models.
\n\n
[Ne V]线强度映射提供了一种强大且统计稳健的方法，可探测包括暗弱、遮蔽及高红移AGN在内的完整AGN群体，超越传统巡天的能力。[Ne V]的高电离势确保了极低的污染，而与星系巡天的交叉关联及红移空间畸变的引入，使得能够获得精确的宇宙学和天体物理约束。未来的[Ne V] LIM实验有望解决若干基本问题，如AGN总辐射历史、低光度端的黑洞-晕质量关系，以及通过区分种子模型来揭示超大质量黑洞的起源。

Abstract: We propose line-intensity mapping (LIM) as a new probe of active galactic nuclei (AGN). By cross-correlating [Ne V] intensity maps with galaxy redshift surveys, we show that the cumulative AGN line emission can be detected even when individual sources are below the detection threshold. The 97.1 eV ionization potential of [Ne V] makes it an essentially uncontaminated tracer of AGN activity, arising from the narrow line region which is accessible even in heavily obscured AGN. We forecast signal-to-noise ratios using a Fisher matrix formalism for two hypothetical future instruments: a CDIM-like instrument targeting [Ne V] $λ3426$ and a PRIMA-like instrument optimized for LIM targeting [Ne V] $14.3 μ$m. For the CDIM-like case we find strong constraints on the product of the mean AGN intensity and bias, $S_{\rm NeV} b_{\rm NeV}$, across $z=2-3$, with redshift-space distortions enabling individual constraints on $S_{\rm NeV}$ and $b_{\rm NeV}$. The LIM signal retains sensitivity to AGN below the $5σ$ direct detection threshold, which at $z=3$ corresponds to $L_{\rm bol} \sim 5\times10^{43}$ erg s$^{-1}$ and coincides with the faint end of existing luminosity function measurements. Roughly 10% of the total signal originates from below this threshold, with the sub-threshold population detectable at $S/N=9-4$ across $z=2-3$ (for $S_{\rm NeV} b_{\rm NeV}$). The PRIMA-like instrument achieves slightly lower signal-to-noise but provides a complementary probe of the AGN population due to the insensitivity of the $14.3 μ$m line to dust attenuation. AGN LIM can potentially be applied to several scientific problems including tracing the total AGN emissivity history, constraining the black hole-halo connection at faint luminosities, and discriminating between supermassive black hole seeding mechanisms.

### [3] [The DESI results impact the local determination of $H_0$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05358)
*Michael S. Turner,Dragan Huterer*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: DESI BAO measurements favor evolving dark energy ($w_0w_a$ models), which reduces the inferred local $H_0$ by up to $2.5\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$ compared to $Λ$CDM; combining with CMB and SNIa data yields smaller but significant shifts ($0.5$–$1.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$), highlighting cosmology-dependence in $H_0$ determinations and its relevance to the Hubble tension.
\n\n
DESI的重子声学振荡（BAO）测量支持演化暗能量（$w_0w_a$模型），这使得局域哈勃常数$H_0$的推断值比$Λ$CDM模型低至多$2.5\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$；结合CMB和SNIa数据后，该偏移减小但仍显著（$0.5$–$1.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$），表明局域$H_0$测定对背景宇宙学模型的依赖性，这对哈勃张力问题具有重要意义。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The Hubble tension—the discrepancy between early- and late-universe determinations of $H_0$—may stem from assumptions about the background cosmological model. This paper investigates whether allowing for evolving dark energy (via $w_0w_a$ parametrization) as suggested by DESI BAO data affects local $H_0$ measurements, thereby probing whether the tension is model-dependent rather than purely observational.
\n\n
哈勃张力——即早期宇宙与晚期宇宙对$H_0$测定之间的不一致——可能源于对背景宇宙学模型的假设。本文探究了DESI BAO数据所支持的演化暗能量（通过$w_0w_a$参数化）是否会影响局域$H_0$的测量，从而检验该张力是否源于模型依赖性而非纯粹的观测差异。

Method: The authors analyze DESI BAO measurements within the $w_0w_a$ dark energy framework, where the equation of state is $w(a) = w_0 + w_a(1 - a)$. They recompute local $H_0$ estimates under this cosmology and combine it with external datasets: Planck CMB and Pantheon+ SNIa. The impact on the inferred $H_0$ value is quantified relative to the $Λ$CDM baseline.
\n\n
作者在$w_0w_a$暗能量框架下分析DESI BAO测量结果，其中状态方程为$w(a) = w_0 + w_a(1 - a)$。他们在该宇宙学模型下重新计算局域$H_0$估计值，并与外部数据集（Planck CMB和Pantheon+ SNIa）联合分析，量化相对于$Λ$CDM基线的$H_0$推断值变化。

Result: Using DESI-preferred $w_0w_a$ models alone lowers local $H_0$ by up to $2.5\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$ versus $Λ$CDM. With CMB constraints, the shift is $1.1 \pm 0.38\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$; adding SNIa further reduces it to $0.5 \pm 0.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$. This demonstrates that low-redshift $H_0$ determinations are sensitive to the assumed background cosmology.
\n\n
仅使用DESI支持的$w_0w_a$模型可使局域$H_0$比$Λ$CDM降低最多$2.5\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$。加入CMB约束后，偏移为$1.1 \pm 0.38\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$；再加入SNIa数据后进一步减小至$0.5 \pm 0.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$。这表明低红移$H_0$测定对所假设的背景宇宙学模型敏感。

Conclusion: The dependence of local $H_0$ measurements on the assumed cosmological model—particularly the dark energy equation of state—suggests that the Hubble tension may be partially alleviated or reinterpreted when non-$Λ$CDM backgrounds are considered. Since low-redshift cosmology is poorly constrained, future analyses must account for this model dependence to avoid biased conclusions.
\n\n
局域$H_0$测量对所假设宇宙学模型（尤其是暗能量状态方程）的依赖性表明，当考虑非$Λ$CDM背景时，哈勃张力可能部分缓解或需重新诠释。由于低红移宇宙学约束较弱，未来分析必须考虑这种模型依赖性，以避免得出有偏的结论。

Abstract: Measurements of baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO) by the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) have revealed evidence for dark energy that evolves. If local distance measurements are analyzed with the $w_0w_a$ models preferred by the DESI measurements, the value for the Hubble constant can be as much as $2.5\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$ smaller than the value obtained assuming $Λ$CDM. When these $w_0w_a$ models are further constrained by cosmic microwave background (CMB) and type Ia supernova (SNIa) data, the downward shift is $1.1 \pm 0.38\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$ (DESI + CMB) and $0.5 \pm 0.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$ (DESI + CMB + SNIa). The dependence of local determinations of $H_0$ on the background cosmology, combined with the fact that the low-redshift cosmology is not well constrained, is relevant to the Hubble tension.
