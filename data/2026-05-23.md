### [1] [Long-lived quasinormal modes of Asymptotically de Sitter Black Holes in Generalized Proca Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21533)
*S. V. Bolokhov*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: Massive scalar perturbations of asymptotically de Sitter black holes in generalized Proca theory exhibit a clear relationship among primary hair, horizon geometry, and scalar field mass. The study uses high-order WKB and time-domain methods to show that as the scalar mass $\\mu$ increases, quasinormal mode frequencies enter a large-mass regime where the real part scales linearly with $\\mu$ and the imaginary part (damping rate) saturates to a constant, ruling out true quasi-resonances. Analytic approximations are derived, and implications for strong cosmic censorship in charged three-horizon spacetimes are discussed.\n\n广义Proca理论中渐近de Sitter黑洞的有质量标量扰动展现出主发（primary hair）、视界结构与场质量之间的清晰相互作用。该研究结合高阶WKB方法和时域演化，表明当标量质量$\\mu$增大时，准正规模频率进入大质量区域：实部随$\\mu$线性增长，虚部（阻尼率）趋于一个非零的几何依赖常数，因此在所研究范围内不会出现真正的准共振。文中还推导了紧致的大$\\mu$解析公式，并讨论了de Sitter类区域对带电三视界时空中的强宇宙监督猜想（strong cosmic censorship）的影响。

Motivation: The paper aims to understand how massive scalar fields behave when perturbing asymptotically de Sitter black holes in generalized Proca theory—a vector-tensor gravity framework that supports primary Proca hair. Conclusion: The absence of true quasi-resonances for massive scalar perturbations in this class of hairy de Sitter black holes suggests robust stability under such perturbations. The derived large-$\\mu$ formula provides a useful analytic tool for future studies. We also identify how the spectrum shifts with black-hole size and Proca hair, derive a compact analytic large-$μ$ formula, and comment on the implications of the de Sitter-like sector for strong cosmic censorship in the charged three-horizon regime.

### [2] [The Relativistic Gravitational Field of a Spherically Symmetric Extended Body](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21551)
*Y. Friedman,S. I. Klimovsky*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: The paper constructs a metric for extended spherically symmetric bodies in Extended Relativity (ER) using a relativistic superposition of retarded gravitational fields. While recovering standard GR predictions at large distances, it reveals weak but non-vanishing dependence on internal mass distribution through higher-order corrections that are significant near compact objects like neutron stars and detectable in precision Earth-orbit experiments.
\n\n
本文在Extended Relativity (ER) 框架下，通过推迟引力场的相对论叠加原理，为延展球对称天体构建了一个度规。该度规在远距离处复现广义相对论（GR）的标准预言，但揭示了外部引力场通过高阶修正微弱地依赖于内部质量分布；这些修正在中子星等致密天体附近显著，并可在地球轨道精密实验中探测到。

Motivation: General Relativity (GR) provides exact vacuum solutions like Schwarzschild for point masses, but realistic astrophysical objects are extended. The Newtonian shell theorem guarantees no external dependence on internal structure, and Birkhoff’s theorem extends this to GR for spherical symmetry. However, in alternative or reformulated theories like Extended Relativity (ER)—which posits gravity as a Lorentz-covariant field on Minkowski spacetime—it is nontrivial whether such theorems hold. The authors aim to explore how internal structure influences the external gravitational field in ER, especially in regimes where relativistic and finite-size effects matter.
\n\n
广义相对论（GR）为质点提供了如Schwarzschild这样的精确真空解，但真实的天体是延展的。牛顿壳层定理保证外部场与内部结构无关，而Birkhoff定理将此性质推广至GR中的球对称情形。然而，在Extended Relativity (ER) 等替代理论或重构理论中——该理论将引力视为闵氏时空上的洛伦兹协变场——此类定理是否成立并不显然。作者旨在探究在ER中内部结构如何影响外部引力场，尤其是在相对论效应和有限尺度效应重要的情形下。

Method: The authors employ the relativistic superposition principle within Extended Relativity (ER), integrating the retarded Liénard–Wiechert-type gravitational potentials from individual mass elements of an extended spherically symmetric source. This yields an explicit metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ expressed as an integral over the source's mass distribution. They expand this metric in powers of $1/r$ to isolate leading-order GR behavior and higher-order structure-dependent corrections. Test-particle geodesics are derived from the admissible-velocity geometry—a concept in ER defining allowed signal speeds—and used to compute observables like light travel time and local light-cone structure.
\n\n
作者在Extended Relativity (ER) 框架内采用相对论叠加原理，对延展球对称源中各质量元产生的推迟型Liénard–Wiechert引力势进行积分，从而得到一个显式度规 $g_{\mu\nu}$，其表达式为对源质量分布的积分。他们将该度规按 $1/r$ 展开，分离出主导阶的GR行为及高阶的结构依赖修正项。通过ER中的admissible-velocity geometry（定义允许信号速度的概念）导出测地线方程，并用于计算光传播时间、局部光锥结构等可观测量。

Result: The derived metric reproduces standard gravitational time dilation and passes classical GR tests in the weak-field limit. Crucially, it exhibits small but non-zero corrections depending on the internal mass distribution (e.g., moments beyond monopole), violating a strict analog of Birkhoff’s theorem. For neutron stars, these corrections alter the local light-velocity structure near the surface. For Earth–ISS round-trip light signals, the model predicts measurable deviations ($\\sim$ picoseconds) from the point-source approximation, potentially testable with current timing technology.
\n\n

Conclusion: Extended Relativity provides a consistent, Lorentz-covariant framework on flat spacetime to describe extended gravitational sources without invoking curved geometry ab initio. The formalism naturally incorporates finite-size relativistic effects, revealing that external fields retain faint imprints of internal structure—unlike in GR due to Birkhoff’s theorem. This opens avenues for testing gravity theories via high-precision measurements sensitive to source structure, particularly in strong-field or near-field regimes.
\n\n
Extended Relativity 在平直时空上提供了一个自洽的、洛伦兹协变的框架，无需预先引入弯曲几何即可描述延展引力源。该形式自然包含了有限尺度的相对论效应，揭示了外部场保留了内部结构的微弱印记——这与GR中因Birkhoff定理而导致的无印记情形不同。这为通过高精度测量（对源结构敏感）检验引力理论开辟了新途径，尤其适用于强场或近场区域。

Abstract: We investigate the gravitational field of an extended spherically symmetric body within the framework of Extended Relativity (ER), a Lorentz-covariant formulation of relativistic gravity on a Minkowski background. Using a relativistic superposition principle for retarded gravitational fields, we derive an explicit metric for an extended body by integrating the contributions of its mass elements.
  The resulting metric reproduces the standard gravitational time dilation of a point source and agrees with the classical tests of General Relativity in the appropriate limits. However, unlike the exact Newtonian shell theorem and the Schwarzschild exterior solution, the external field depends weakly on the internal mass distribution through higher-order corrections. These corrections decay rapidly with distance but become significant near compact objects.
  We analyze the corresponding admissible-velocity geometry, derive the motion equations for test particles, and compare the predictions of extended-body and point-source models. For neutron stars, the corrections noticeably modify the local light-velocity structure near the surface. For the Earth, the corrections are small but produce measurable differences in round-trip light travel times to the International Space Station.

### [3] [Astrophysical Objects in Modified Theories of Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21564)
*Sneha Pradhan*

\n\n

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: General Relativity (GR) faces theoretical and observational challenges at extreme densities found in compact stars, motivating extensions like $f(T)$ and $f(Q)$ gravity—formulated via teleparallelism using torsion or nonmetricity instead of curvature. Understanding how these theories affect the structure and stability of neutron and strange stars is essential for testing gravity beyond GR and interpreting multimessenger astrophysical data.
\n\n
广义相对论（GR）在致密星内部极端密度条件下面临理论和观测挑战，促使人们发展如$f(T)$和$f(Q)$引力等扩展理论——这些理论基于挠率或非度规性的平移对称性（teleparallelism），而非曲率。理解这些理论如何影响中子星和奇异星的结构与稳定性，对于检验GR之外的引力理论并解释多信使天体物理数据至关重要。

Abstract: This chapter reviews the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques to the study of galaxy clusters, covering both theoretical developments and their use as tools to infer cluster properties from a variety of observational tracers. We discuss recent advances in mass estimation from SZ, X-ray, optical, and dynamical data, highlighting the ability of AI methods to capture non-linear features, projection effects, and complex cluster morphologies beyond more classical approaches. In addition, we present other emerging applications, including the emulation of baryonic physics from N-body simulations, the characterization of dynamical states and mergers, and the analysis of the diffuse components such as the intracluster light. Particular emphasis is placed on the role of simulations in training these models, the impact of baryonic modelling, and the need for a robust uncertainty quantification and interpretability. Finally, we outline current limitations and future prospects, stressing the importance of combining flexible simulation strategies with AI techniques to fully exploit next-generation surveys for precision cosmology.
