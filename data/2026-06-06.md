*Shahnawaz A. Adil,Sonej Alam,Somasri Sen,J. Alberto Vazquez*

本文研究了一种基于体粘滞（bulk viscosity）的暗能量模型，表明其能有效模拟动力学暗能量，并在包含非最小耦合与相互作用的情形下，比$\\Lambda$CDM更好地拟合宇宙学观测数据。

### [3] [Reconstructing dark energy with fewer assumptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05853)
*Daniel A. Kessler,Eleonora Di Valentino,Luis A. Escamilla,Dragan Huterer*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: The paper presents a minimal, assumption-averse reconstruction of dark energy density and equation of state using late-time distance data (BAO from DESI/SDSS and SNe Ia from Pantheon+, Union3.1, DES-Dovekie), binned into seven redshift intervals up to $z=4.2$. Results consistently show a non-monotonic dark energy evolution with a local maximum in density and oscillatory behavior in the equation of state around $w=-1$, including a tentative phantom crossing at $z\sim0.6$–$0.8$. These features are robust under extensions (e.g., curvature, neutrino mass) and localized principal component analysis, with deviations from $Λ$CDM reaching $\sim2.6$–$3σ$ per bin but only $\sim2σ$ overall support for extra parameters. The main conclusion is the remarkable consistency across independent datasets, suggesting a coherent signal beyond statistical fluctuations or systematics.
\n\n
本文采用极简且避免假设的方法，利用晚期宇宙距离测量数据（DESI/SDSS的BAO和Pantheon+、Union3.1、DES-Dovekie的Ia型超新星），在红移区间$z=0$到$z=4.2$内划分为七个红移bin，重构暗能量密度和状态方程。结果一致显示非单调的暗能量演化：密度先升至局部极大值后下降，状态方程在宇宙学常数极限$w=-1$附近呈现两次明显振荡，并在$z\sim0.6$–$0.8$处存在穿越phantom界限的迹象。这些特征在引入空间曲率、中微子质量等参数扩展以及局域主成分分析（localized principal component analysis）下依然稳健。单个bin对$Λ$CDM的偏离显著性达$\sim2.6$–$3σ$，但整体卡方差异仅提供$\sim2σ$的支持以引入七个额外参数。主要结论是：多种主流背景观测数据高度一致，表明暗能量演化信号是数据中的持续特征，不能仅归因于个别测量的涨落或系统误差。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to test the $Λ$CDM paradigm without imposing strong theoretical priors on dark energy evolution. Our results suggest that the dark energy evolution signal is a persistent feature of the data and that it cannot be explained solely by fluctuations or systematics in individual measurements.

Main category: astro-ph.CO

\n\n
从带掩模的宇宙剪切星表中重建高保真质量图面临巡天边界、空间变化噪声以及在弯曲天球上高分辨率计算瓶颈的挑战。此前如 AKRA 2.0 的方法需显式构造并求逆稠密的法方程矩阵 $\mathbf{H}$，其内存和时间复杂度分别为 $O(N^2)$ 和 $O(N^3)$，导致对 Stage~III/IV 巡天而言，全天空高分辨率重建不可行。
