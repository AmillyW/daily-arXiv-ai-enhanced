### [1] [Hypergroup Symmetry in Relative Quantum Field Theories and Chiral Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05279)
*Terry Gannon,Brandon C. Rayhaun*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: This paper develops a framework for noninvertible symmetries in relative 2D quantum field theories (QFTs)—those living at the boundary of a 3D topological QFT—and introduces new algebraic structures like dome algebras and hypergroups. It establishes a correspondence between finite symmetries and conformal embeddings in rational chiral algebras, and derives implications for absolute 2D CFTs, including a gluing procedure for chiral symmetries into topological line defects and a precise link between boundary conditions and chiral symmetries, with consequences for Cardy states and $g$-functions.
\n\n
本文发展了一套用于处理相对二维量子场论（relative 2D QFT）中非可逆对称性的通用框架——这类理论位于三维拓扑量子场论（topological QFT）的边界上，并引入了如dome代数和hypergroup等新代数结构。文章建立了有理共形代数中有限对称性与有限指标共形嵌入之间的一一对应关系，并推导出该框架对绝对二维共形场论（2D CFT）的若干推论：包括将左右手共形代数的对称性“粘合”为整体理论的拓扑线缺陷的方法，以及边界条件与共形代数对称性之间的精确对应关系，后者在对角有理CFT中导出了拓扑线缺陷在边界条件上的传递作用，以及恒等Cardy态具有最小$g$-函数的结论。

They introduce "dome algebras" as generalizations of tube algebras to capture the fusion and action of noninvertible symmetries. The analysis leverages the structure of modular tensor categories, conformal embeddings, and the relationship between chiral algebras and their fixed-point subalgebras under hypergroup actions. For absolute theories, they use the factorization of the full CFT into left- and right-moving chiral sectors to construct topological line defects via symmetry gluing.
\n\n
作者采用基于拓扑量子场论和有理共形场论的范畴与代数方法。他们将相对二维QFT建模为三维拓扑QFT的边界条件，利用体态中的拓扑面算子来定义边界上的对称性作用。他们引入“dome代数”作为tube代数的推广，以刻画非可逆对称性的融合与作用。分析中利用了模张量范畴的结构、共形嵌入，以及共形代数与其在hypergroup作用下的不动点子代数之间的关系。对于绝对理论，他们利用完整CFT可分解为左右手共形扇区的性质，通过对称性“粘合”来构造拓扑线缺陷。

Result: Key results include: (1) a one-to-one correspondence between finite symmetries of a rational chiral algebra and conformal embeddings of finite index; (2) a gluing prescription that combines symmetries of left- and right-moving chiral algebras into topological line defects of the full 2D CFT; (3) a precise bijection between boundary conditions of a 2D CFT and symmetries of its chiral algebra; (4) in diagonal rational CFTs, topological line defects act transitively on boundary conditions, and the identity Cardy state minimizes the $g$-function even among Virasoro-preserving boundaries; (5) a constraint on potential $c=8$ chiral algebras with Haagerup-related modular data, requiring them to be fixed points of a rank-2 hypergroup acting on $SU(3)_1 \\otimes (E_6)_1$.

The work bridges concepts from topological field theory, category theory, and conformal field theory, offering new tools for exploring non-Lagrangian and non-group-like symmetries.
\n\n
本文为相对二维QFT中的非可逆对称性建立了一个坚实的理论框架，揭示了体态拓扑数据、边界对称结构与共形代数表示论之间的深刻联系。研究表明，相对理论为理解hypergroup等奇异对称结构提供了自然舞台，而这些结构对绝对CFT（尤其是在边界条件与拓扑缺陷的分类方面）具有具体且可检验的推论。该工作融合了拓扑场论、范畴论与共形场论的概念，为探索非拉格朗日型及非群型对称性提供了新工具。

Abstract: A QFT is said to be relative if it lives at the boundary of a topological QFT in one higher dimension. We develop a general framework for working with noninvertible symmetries of relative theories in two spacetime dimensions, extending several well-known results for absolute QFTs. We emphasize various new features which arise in the relative setting, including the role of topological surfaces of the bulk, and the appearance of hypergroups and certain generalizations of tube algebras known as dome algebras. Our formalism is particularly well-suited for studying rational chiral algebras, where it predicts that finite symmetries are in explicit one-to-one correspondence with conformal embeddings of finite index.
  We describe several implications of our framework for absolute theories. First, we explain how to "glue" together symmetries of the left- and right-moving chiral algebras of a 2D CFT to produce topological line defects of the full theory. Second, we derive a precise correspondence between boundary conditions of a 2D CFT and symmetries of its chiral algebra. This correspondence has several structural corollaries: in diagonal rational CFTs, we demonstrate that the topological line defects of the theory act transitively on its boundary conditions, and further that the identity Cardy state has the smallest $g$-function amongst all boundary conditions, including those which only preserve Virasoro symmetry.

For instance, we show that, if there exists a rational chiral algebra with central charge $c=8$ whose modular tensor category is the Drinfeld center of the Haagerup fusion category, then it must arise as the fixed points of a rank-2 hypergroup acting on the $SU(3)_1\otimes (E_{6})_1$ chiral algebra.

### [2] [The Double Well Done Doubly-Well](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05282)
*Aurélien Dersy,Matthew D. Schwartz*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: This paper provides a detailed, self-contained analysis of the trans-series structure of the energy spectrum in the symmetric quantum double-well potential using two complementary frameworks: exact WKB theory and the Euclidean path integral. Both approaches reveal how resurgence connects perturbative and non-perturbative contributions—factorially divergent series and exponentially small instanton effects—through Stokes phenomena and elliptic curve geometry, with explicit computations up to four-instanton and three-loop orders.
\n\n
本文通过两种互补的框架——exact WKB理论和Euclidean路径积分——对对称量子双阱势能中能谱的trans-series结构进行了详细且自洽的分析。两种方法均揭示了resurgence如何通过Stokes现象和椭圆曲线几何将微扰与非微扰贡献（阶乘发散级数与指数小的instanton效应）联系起来，并给出了直至四instanton和三圈阶的具体计算。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The symmetric double-well potential serves as a paradigmatic model where perturbative expansions fail to capture key non-perturbative features like parity splitting, which is exponentially suppressed. Understanding how these disparate sectors unify into a consistent physical prediction is central to the theory of resurgence. The paper aims to clarify this unification by explicitly constructing the full trans-series for the energy levels using two distinct but mathematically related methods, thereby illuminating the role of global analytic structure and saddle-point topology.
\n\n
对称双阱势是一个典型模型，其中微扰展开无法捕捉诸如宇称分裂等关键的非微扰特征（因其指数压低）。理解这些不同部分如何统一为一致的物理预言是resurgence理论的核心。本文旨在通过使用两种不同但数学上相关的显式方法构建能级的完整trans-series，从而阐明全局解析结构和鞍点拓扑的作用。

Method: The authors employ two complementary techniques: (1) exact WKB analysis, where the quantization condition is formulated via Voros symbols and analytic continuation across turning points is governed by Delabaere–Dillinger–Pham (DDP) relations encoding Stokes phenomena; and (2) Euclidean path integral formalism, where classical solutions are doubly-periodic elliptic functions, and the partition function’s trans-series is derived via Lefschetz thimble decomposition over quasi-zero modes. Both methods are developed in a unified notation and carried out explicitly to four-instanton order and three-loop accuracy.
\n\n
作者采用两种互补的技术：(1) exact WKB分析，其中量子化条件通过Voros符号表达，而穿过转折点的解析延拓由编码Stokes现象的Delabaere–Dillinger–Pham（DDP）关系控制；(2) Euclidean路径积分形式，其中经典解是Euclidean时间的双周期椭圆函数，配分函数的trans-series通过在准零模（quasi-zero modes）上的Lefschetz thimble分解导出。两种方法均采用统一记号，并显式计算至四instanton阶和三圈精度。

In the exact WKB approach, the DDP relations—local in nature—suffice to determine the global monodromy without full knowledge of the elliptic curve’s topology. In the path integral, the trans-series for the partition function exhibits polynomial $T$-dependence at each instanton order, dictated by finite-dimensional thimble integrals over quasi-zero modes. Both frameworks rely on the same underlying elliptic curve structure but access it through different mathematical mechanisms.
\n\n
本文构建了直至四instanton阶的完整能级trans-series，展示了resurgence如何将大阶微扰理论（具有阶乘增长系数）与多instanton振幅联系起来。在exact WKB方法中，局部性质的DDP关系足以确定全局单值性，无需完全了解椭圆曲线的拓扑。在路径积分中，配分函数的trans-series在每个instanton阶呈现多项式形式的$T$依赖性，由准零模上的有限维thimble积分决定。两种框架依赖相同的椭圆曲线结构，但通过不同的数学机制加以利用。

Conclusion: The symmetric double-well system exemplifies how resurgence unifies perturbative and non-perturbative physics into a single trans-series governed by analytic continuation, Stokes phenomena, and elliptic geometry. The convergence of results from exact WKB and path integral methods—despite their differing treatments of the same underlying structure—validates the robustness of resurgence and positions the double well as an ideal theoretical laboratory for exploring non-perturbative quantum mechanics.
\n\n
对称双阱系统典型地展示了resurgence如何通过解析延拓、Stokes现象和椭圆几何将微扰与非微扰物理统一到单一的trans-series中。尽管exact WKB与路径积分方法对相同底层结构的处理方式不同，但其结果的一致性验证了resurgence的稳健性，并确立双阱系统作为探索非微扰量子力学的理想理论实验室。

Abstract: The symmetric double-well potential is one of the simplest quantum-mechanical systems in which perturbative and non-perturbative physics are deeply entangled. Its energy levels have non-analytic expansions in inverse powers of the inter-well separation, with factorially growing coefficients, while the parity splitting is exponentially small and invisible to perturbation theory. Resurgence ties the two features together, organizing the exact spectrum into a single tightly-constrained trans-series. A Lefschetz thimble decomposition determines which saddles contribute, and the resulting partition function trans-series is much simpler than the energy trans-series: at each instanton order the $T$-dependence is a polynomial fixed by the quasi-zero-mode thimble integrals. Together, the two approaches deploy a shared mathematical infrastructure in complementary ways, showing that the double well is an ideal setting to explore resurgence.
