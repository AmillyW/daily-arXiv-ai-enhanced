### [1] [A No-Go Theorem for the Mass-Radius Relation of Solitons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22901)
*Mohammad Hossein Namjoo*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: The paper establishes a no-go theorem stating that stable, localized, non-topological solitons in real scalar field theories (and analogous self-gravitating barotropic fluids) in $d$ spatial dimensions cannot have a mass-radius index $Γ = \frac{d\ln M}{d\ln R}$ within the interval $[0, d]$, under generic conditions excluding fine-tuned force balance. This excludes natural ultra-light or fluid-like dark matter models as explanations for observed dark matter halo cores with $Γ \simeq 1.7$ in $d=3$.
\n\n
本文建立了一个“禁戒定理”（no-go theorem），指出在$d$维空间中，实标量场理论中的稳定、局域化、非拓扑孤子（以及类似的自引力多方流体）在一般条件下（排除三种力精细平衡的参数区域）不可能具有质量-半径指数$Γ = \\frac{d\\ln M}{d\\ln R}$落在区间$[0, d]$内。这一结论排除了超轻暗物质或类流体暗物质模型作为观测到的暗物质晕核（$d=3$时$Γ \\simeq 1.7$）的自然解释。

Motivation: The motivation stems from the tension between theoretical models of dark matter—particularly ultra-light scalar field dark matter and fluid-like models—that predict solitonic cores in dark matter halos, and astrophysical observations suggesting such cores have a mass-radius relation with $Γ \simeq 1.7$. The authors aim to determine whether such a scaling is theoretically viable for stable, non-relativistic, spherically symmetric solitons without fine-tuning, especially given the generality of possible self-interactions and density profiles.
\n\n

Method: The authors define the mass-radius index $Γ \equiv \frac{d\ln M}{d\ln R}$ and analyze the virial equilibrium of solitons governed by three competing forces: gradient energy, self-interaction potential, and Newtonian gravity. They employ a general variational or scaling argument (akin to Derrick’s theorem but extended to include gravity and arbitrary potentials) to derive constraints on $Γ$. Crucially, they assume “typicality”—that not all three forces are simultaneously comparable in magnitude—thereby excluding finely tuned parameter regions. The analysis accommodates arbitrary self-interactions (non-power-law, non-analytic at vacuum, derivative couplings) and makes no assumptions about the soliton profile or functional form of $Γ(R)$.
\n\n
作者定义质量-半径指数$Γ \\equiv \\frac{d\\ln M}{d\\ln R}$，并分析由三种竞争作用力（梯度能、自相互作用势和牛顿引力）主导的孤子的位力平衡。他们采用一种广义的变分或标度论证（类似于Derrick定理，但扩展至包含引力和任意势能），推导出对$Γ$的约束。关键假设是“典型性”（typicality）——即三种力并非同时具有可比拟的强度，从而排除精细调节的参数区域。该分析适用于任意自相互作用（非幂律、在真空处非解析、含导数耦合），且不对孤子密度轮廓或$Γ(R)$的函数形式作任何假设。

Result: The main result is a rigorous exclusion: for stable, localized, non-topological solitons in $d$ spatial dimensions composed of real scalar fields (or self-gravitating barotropic fluids), the mass-radius index $Γ$ cannot lie in the closed interval $[0, d]$ under typical (non-fine-tuned) conditions. This applies regardless of the form of the self-interaction or density profile. For $d=3$, the observationally favored value $Γ \simeq 1.7$ falls squarely within this forbidden range, ruling out natural realizations of ultra-light scalar or fluid dark matter as the origin of halo cores if other astrophysical effects are negligible.
\n\n
主要结果是一个严格的排除结论：在$d$维空间中，由实标量场（或自引力多方流体）构成的稳定、局域化、非拓扑孤子，在典型（非精细调节）条件下，其质量-半径指数$Γ$不可能落在闭区间$[0, d]$内。该结论不依赖于自相互作用或密度轮廓的具体形式。对于$d=3$，观测偏好的值$Γ \\simeq 1.7$正好位于该禁戒区间内，因此若其他天体物理效应可忽略，则超轻标量场或类流体暗物质模型无法自然地解释暗物质晕核的起源。

Conclusion: The no-go theorem demonstrates a fundamental incompatibility between the observed scaling of dark matter halo cores ($Γ \simeq 1.7$) and the theoretical expectations for stable solitonic solutions in generic real scalar field theories or barotropic fluid models. This strongly disfavors ultra-light or fluid-like dark matter as natural explanations for core formation, unless significant fine-tuning or additional astrophysical mechanisms (e.g., baryonic feedback) are invoked.
\n\n
该禁戒定理揭示了观测到的暗物质晕核标度律（$Γ \\simeq 1.7$）与通用实标量场理论或多方流体模型中稳定孤子解的理论预期之间存在根本性不兼容。除非引入显著的精细调节或其他天体物理机制（如重子反馈），否则超轻或类流体暗物质模型难以作为晕核形成的自然解释。

Abstract: We prove a no-go theorem for the mass-radius relation of localized and stable field configurations, known as solitons. Defining the mass-radius index by $Γ\equiv \frac{\rm{d}\ln M}{\rm{d}\ln R}$, for real scalar field theories in $d$ spatial dimensions, we show that typical non-topological, non-relativistic, and spherically symmetric solitons cannot have $Γ$ in the range $[0, d]$. The forces considered originate from gradient energy, self-interaction, and gravitation, with the typicality assumption excluding the fine-tuned region of the parameter space where all three forces have comparable strength. Importantly, the theorem works for an arbitrary self-interaction that, in the relativistic theory, is allowed to be non-power-law in the field, be non-analytic around the classical vacuum (where the field amplitude vanishes), or to include derivative couplings. Additionally, the theorem makes no assumptions about the explicit form of the soliton's density profile or the behavior of $Γ$ as a function of $R$. We also argue that the same exclusion applies to compact objects formed from self-gravitating, non-relativistic, barotropic fluids with arbitrary equations of state. As a consequence for cosmology, it is worth noting that observations favor a core in the centers of dark matter halos with $Γ\simeq 1.7$, which (for $d=3$) lies approximately in the middle of the excluded range. Therefore, proposals such as ultra-light or fluid-like dark matter models are essentially ruled out as natural explanations for halo cores, provided other astrophysical effects are negligible.

### [2] [The $τ$ of Neutral Hydrogen: Increased CMB Optical Depth at Long Wavelengths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22934)
*Gilbert Holder,Adrian Liu,Tzu-Ching Chang,David Zegeye*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: This paper proposes that CMB photons at wavelengths longer than 21 cm can be absorbed by neutral hydrogen during the cosmic "dark ages," leading to a measurable suppression in CMB clustering. This effect, dependent on the spin temperature $T_s$ and neutral fraction $x_{HI}$, may be detectable via cross-correlation with high-frequency CMB maps, offering a novel probe of $x_{HI}/T_s(z)$.
\n\n
本文提出，在宇宙“黑暗时代”，波长大于21 cm的CMB光子可被中性氢吸收，导致CMB成团性出现可观测的抑制效应。该效应依赖于自旋温度 $T_s$ 和中性分数 $x_{HI}$，通过与高频CMB图的交叉关联可能被探测到，从而提供一种测量 $x_{HI}/T_s(z)$ 的新方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to explore a previously underappreciated absorption effect on the CMB at long radio wavelengths due to neutral hydrogen during the pre-reionization era. Standard CMB analyses focus on Thomson scattering, but this work highlights an additional opacity source tied to the 21 cm physics, which encodes information about the thermal and ionization history of the early Universe.
\n\n
动机在于探索再电离前时期中性氢对长波射电波段CMB的一种此前被低估的吸收效应。标准CMB分析聚焦于Thomson散射，而本工作强调了一种与21 cm物理相关的额外光学深度来源，其中编码了早期宇宙热历史和电离历史的信息。

Method: The authors compute the frequency-dependent optical depth $\tau(\nu)$ arising from resonant absorption of CMB photons by neutral hydrogen in the hyperfine transition, which depends on the spin temperature $T_s(z)$ and neutral fraction $x_{HI}(z)$. They estimate the resulting suppression in CMB anisotropy power spectra and propose using cross-correlation between low-frequency (radio) and high-frequency (mm-wave) CMB maps to isolate this signal from foregrounds and instrumental noise.
\n\n
作者计算了由中性氢超精细跃迁对CMB光子的共振吸收所引起的频率相关光学深度 $\tau(\nu)$，其依赖于自旋温度 $T_s(z)$ 和中性分数 $x_{HI}(z)$。他们估算了由此导致的CMB各向异性功率谱的抑制，并提出通过低频（射电）与高频（毫米波）CMB图的交叉关联来从前景和仪器噪声中提取该信号。

Result: The excess optical depth peaks at $\sim$100 MHz with amplitude of a few percent, imprinting a redshift-dependent suppression on CMB fluctuations. Cross-correlating radio-wavelength CMB data with existing mm-wave CMB maps could yield a detection of this effect even if the intrinsic 21 cm signal remains undetected. The amplitude of the suppression directly probes the ratio $x_{HI}/T_s$ as a function of redshift.
\n\n
额外光学深度在约100 MHz处达到峰值，幅度为百分之几，对CMB涨落造成红移依赖的抑制。即使本征21 cm信号尚未被探测到，将射电波段CMB数据与现有毫米波CMB图交叉关联仍可能探测到该效应。该抑制幅度直接探测红移相关的比值 $x_{HI}/T_s$。

Conclusion: This absorption effect offers a new observational window into the dark ages through CMB measurements alone, complementary to traditional 21 cm experiments. By leveraging cross-correlation techniques, it may provide a more robust path to constraining the evolution of $x_{HI}$ and $T_s$ during cosmic dawn and the dark ages.
\n\n
该吸收效应通过纯CMB测量为“黑暗时代”提供了新的观测窗口，是对传统21 cm实验的补充。借助交叉关联技术，它可能为约束宇宙黎明和黑暗时代期间 $x_{HI}$ 与 $T_s$ 的演化提供更稳健的途径。

Abstract: At wavelengths longer than 21 cm, photons from the long-wavelength tail of the cosmic microwave background (CMB) have a non-zero probability of being absorbed by distant neutral hydrogen. This provides an additional suppression of the observed CMB clustering in addition to the usual Thomson scattering. The optical depth as a function of frequency is sensitive to the 21 cm spin temperature $T_s$ of the gas as a function of cosmic time, with the excess optical depth peaking at a level of a few percent around 100 MHz. It is likely difficult to detect the CMB at these long radio wavelengths, but the cause is aided by the ability to cross-correlate with the already well-characterized fluctuations at cm/mm frequencies. We find that detecting CMB fluctuations at radio wavelengths corresponding to the 21 cm ``dark ages'' in cross-correlation with mm-wave maps may be easier than detecting the intrinsic 21 cm fluctuations. Measurement of the amplitude of CMB fluctuations as a function of radio wavelength provides a path for a new type of direct measurement of the combination $x_{HI}/T_s$ as a function of redshift.
