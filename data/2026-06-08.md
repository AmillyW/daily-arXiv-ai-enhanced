### [1] [Impact of fiducial cosmology in model-agnostic cosmological inference with the BAO feature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06591)
*Aseem Paranjape,Ravi K. Sheth*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: The paper completes the 'Zel'dovich smearing approximation'—a model-agnostic framework for BAO cosmological inference in redshift space—by incorporating geometric distortions from fiducial cosmology choices. It validates the framework on AbacusSummit halo mocks mimicking DESI and Euclid, showing unbiased constraints and enabling future performance comparisons with traditional template-based methods.
\n\n

This work aims to incorporate that missing ingredient to make the framework fully self-consistent and applicable to real data.

We end with a roadmap to a detailed comparison of the performance of the traditional approach with the Zel'dovich smearing approximation, which is `data ready' as presented here.

### [3] [Halo mass functions in mixed cold and fuzzy dark matter models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06599)
*Sarah C. Johnston,Simon May,Tibor Dome,Sownak Bose,Alastair Basden,Carlton Baugh,Anastasia Fialkov,Alex Tocher*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

They observe that FDM suppresses small-scale structure, reducing low-mass halo abundance in a redshift- and $f$-dependent way, and shifts/modifies the HMF slope at high masses. A phenomenological suppression model is proposed that maps CDM HMFs to MDM HMFs with ~0.1–0.2 dex accuracy over $1 \leq z \leq 4$, $f \leq 0.3$, enabling fast predictions without new simulations.
\n\n
本文利用AxiREPO框架的数值模拟研究了混合冷暗物质与模糊暗物质（MDM）宇宙学模型，重点关注超轻类轴子FDM组分（质量 $m = 10^{-24.5} \mathrm{eV}$，占比 $f \leq 0.3$）对晕质量函数（HMF）的影响。作者开发了一种新颖的基于网格的晕搜寻算法，将粒子型冷暗物质（CDM）与波动型模糊暗物质（FDM）统一为单一密度场以实现一致的晕识别。他们发现FDM通过波干涉效应抑制小尺度结构，以依赖于红移和 $f$ 的方式减少低质量晕的数量，并系统性地使HMF整体下移并改变其高质量端斜率。作者提出一个唯象压制函数模型，可在 $1 \leq z \leq 4$、$f \leq 0.3$ 参数空间内以约0.1–0.2 dex的精度将CDM HMF映射到MDM HMF，从而无需为每个参数重新进行模拟即可快速预测结构形成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation stems from the need to understand how mixed dark matter scenarios—specifically those combining standard cold dark matter (CDM) with ultralight fuzzy dark matter (FDM)—affect cosmic structure formation, particularly the halo mass function (HMF). While pure FDM models are tightly constrained by observations of small-scale structure, mixed models with subdominant FDM fractions remain viable and can alleviate some tensions of CDM (e.g., missing satellites). \n\n
本文为混合冷暗物质与模糊暗物质宇宙学中的结构形成模拟与分析建立了稳健的方法论。通过引入基于统一密度场的晕搜寻算法和经过校准的HMF压制唯象模型，该工作能够在无需为每组参数重新进行模拟的情况下，高效且准确地预测MDM场景中的晕统计特性。该框架为未来利用星系巡天、Lyman-$\\alpha$ 森林数据和强引力透镜对MDM模型进行观测检验铺平了道路，并为将晕建模推广至其他多组分暗物质理论提供了模板。

### [4] [Inverse-Scattering Reconstruction of Inflation from Scalar and Tensor Primordial Spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06900)
*Jorge Mastache,Allan Hurtado*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: The paper introduces an inverse-scattering approach to reconstruct effective inflationary potentials from primordial power spectra by mapping the Mukhanov–Sasaki equation to a Schrödinger problem, identifying the Bunch–Davies vacuum with the Jost solution, and expressing scalar and tensor spectra via Jost functions. The method successfully recovers smooth slow-roll potentials and reveals limitations near sharp features.
\n\n
本文提出一种逆散射方法，通过将Mukhanov–Sasaki方程映射为半直线上的Schrödinger型问题，将Bunch–Davies真空识别为Jost解，并用Jost函数表达标量与张量原初功率谱，从而从观测谱重构有效暴胀势。该方法能准确复现平滑慢滚势的主导行为，但在存在尖锐特征时揭示出标量势重构的局限性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to establish a direct, physically transparent link between observed features in the primordial power spectra and the underlying inflationary dynamics, especially beyond the slow-roll approximation. Traditional forward modeling is often indirect; inverse scattering offers a non-perturbative framework to infer the effective potentials $z''/z$ (for scalars) and $a''/a$ (for tensors) directly from spectral data.
\n\n
动机在于建立原初功率谱中观测特征与底层暴胀动力学之间的直接且物理清晰的联系，尤其是在慢滚近似之外的情形。传统的正向建模通常间接；而逆散射提供了一个非微扰框架，可直接从谱数据推断标量的有效势 $z''/z$ 和张量的有效势 $a''/a$。

Method: The authors recast the Mukhanov–Sasaki equation for curvature and tensor perturbations as a time-independent Schrödinger equation on the half-line $(-\infty, 0)$ with effective potentials $V_s(\eta) = z''/z$ and $V_t(\eta) = a''/a$. They identify the Bunch–Davies initial condition with the Jost solution asymptotic to $e^{-ik\eta}$ as $\eta \to -\infty$, and express the freeze-out amplitude of the growing mode via the Jost function $F_{\nu - 1/2}(k)$. The primordial power spectra are then written as $\\mathcal{P}(k) \\propto 1/|F(k)|^2$. Using inverse-scattering theory, specifically the Marchenko equation linearized via the Born approximation in the large-$k$ limit, they reconstruct $V_s$ and $V_t$ from synthetic spectra generated by known benchmark potentials (quadratic and step-like).
\n\n
作者将曲率扰动和张量扰动的Mukhanov–Sasaki方程重写为定义在半直线 $(-\infty, 0)$ 上的定态Schrödinger方程，其有效势分别为 $V_s(\eta) = z''/z$（标量）和 $V_t(\eta) = a''/a$（张量）。他们将Bunch–Davies初始条件识别为当 $\eta \to -\infty$ 时渐近于 $e^{-ik\eta}$ 的Jost解，并通过Jost函数 $F_{\nu - 1/2}(k)$ 表达增长模在冻结时刻的振幅。原初功率谱由此写为 $\\mathcal{P}(k) \\propto 1/|F(k)|^2$。利用逆散射理论，特别是在大 $k$ 极限下通过Born近似线性化的Marchenko方程，他们从已知基准势（二次势和阶跃势）生成的合成谱中重构出 $V_s$ 和 $V_t$。

Result: For a smooth quadratic potential, the reconstructed effective potentials accurately reproduce the dominant behavior of $z''/z$ and $a''/a$. Abstract: During cosmological inflation, inhomogeneities arising from quantum vacuum fluctuations are stretched to become super-Hubble and effectively classical. As many scenarios of the origin involve nonlinearities or a breakdown of perturbativity in the infrared, the limitations of quantum field theory can be addressed using a stochastic description of the dynamics, the so-called stochastic inflation paradigm. However, the stochastic formalism was only recently formulated consistently within full General Relativity and has not yet been extended to more general theories of the early universe, which is the subject of this work.
  In order to find the stochastic sources for a wide class of fully nonlinear scalar-tensor theories, we apply our gauge-agnostic coarse-graining procedure to the linear equations of the effective field theory of dark energy. Each theory can then be mapped to its own set of stochastic equations of motion by identifying the corresponding coefficients in the EFT. We illustrate this with a few concrete and, in most cases, unprecedented examples, including Gauss-Bonnet, generalized Brans-Dicke, Horndeski, and braiding theories.
  Finally, we discuss other natural extensions to provide a phenomenologically complete stochastic framework. For example, we showcase the coarse-graining of multifield inflation in full General Relativity and argue for the generality of our procedure and thus its potential applications beyond the realm of inflation.
